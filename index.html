FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Ask on Telegram; there’s no obligation.

Are the projects on this site real clients?

No. They’re concept projects we designed and built ourselves to show our range across different industries. They are fully working sites you can open and test. We prefer to be upfront rather than show made‑up client logos or reviews.

What do I need to provide?

Your logo (if you have one), a short description of your business and services, any photos you’d like to use, and examples of sites you like. If you don’t have copy or images, we can help with English copywriting and quality stock photography.

How does payment work?

After you approve the fixed quote, 50% is paid to start and the remaining 50% on completion, before the site goes live. The payment method is agreed with you on Telegram.

Do I need a server or database?

Usually not. Most small business sites work perfectly as fast static websites, which can be hosted for free or very cheaply (e.g. GitHub Pages, Netlify or Cloudflare Pages). If you need a store, booking or a CMS, we connect a trusted hosted service instead of building a fragile backend.

What counts as a revision round?

One round is a single consolidated list of changes you send us, such as text edits, layout tweaks or colour adjustments. Major changes in scope (for example adding new pages) are quoted separately and agreed first.

Will I be able to update the site myself?

Yes. We hand over clean, well‑organised files and a short guide. If you prefer not to touch it, our optional monthly maintenance add‑on covers small updates for you.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Absolutely. Send us the link and tell us what isn’t working. We’ll suggest improvements and quote a fixed price for the redesign.

Who owns the website when it’s finished?

You do. Once the final payment is made, you receive all source files and full rights to use them for your business.
